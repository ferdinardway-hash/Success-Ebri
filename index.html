A little about me

The person behind the strategy

I did not plan to fall in love with social media strategy. It started with one client, one late night, and a content calendar built purely on instinct. When her bookings picked up, I knew I had stumbled onto something worth taking seriously. So I did, properly this time: the psychology, the systems, the ads, all of it.

The belief that shaped everything since has not changed. Social media exists to connect people, not to perform for an algorithm. Every strategy I build starts with one question: who is actually on the other side of the screen, and what would make them stop scrolling and start trusting you?

These days my best work goes to founder-led wellness and ADHD coaches, brilliant with their clients, quietly unsure how to sound like themselves online. Startups, solo founders, growing practices, it does not matter the stage. That gap between what you know and how you show up online is exactly what I close.

Outside of strategy decks, I am the kind of creative who gets attached to small details other people would skip past. If you want predictable and forgettable, I am not your person. If you want someone who actually sits with your brand and comes back with something that works and does not sound like everyone else's content, we will get along well.

"Your brand does not need to shout. It needs to sound like you, on purpose, every single time."
