About Me

Who is Success Ebri?

I did not plan to fall in love with social media strategy. It started with one client, one late night, and a content calendar built purely on instinct. When her bookings picked up, I knew I had stumbled onto something worth taking seriously.

These days my best work goes to founder-led wellness and ADHD coaches, brilliant with their clients, quietly unsure how to sound like themselves online. Startups, solo founders, growing practices, it does not matter the stage.

1

Book a call

2

Market & competitor research

I look into your space, your audience, and who else is competing for their attention.

3

Messaging foundation

Marketing strategy

A clear plan mapped to your goals, channel by channel, so nothing is left to guesswork.
